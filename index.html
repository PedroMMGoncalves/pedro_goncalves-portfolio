Mineral Resources and Geophysics, Research Unit, Portugal

As a Senior Technician at the National Laboratory of Energy and Geology (LNEG) since August 2019 , and previously as a Research Fellow starting in 2013 , I have played a key role in the technical development of numerous national and international research projects. My functions involve utilizing Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and applying a multi-scale sensor approach, from satellite to drone (sUASA) surveys, for mineral exploration and geological characterization. I have contributed to the creation of critical cartography, such as the Critical Raw Materials Deposits Map of mainland Portugal, and have been involved in processing geological and geochemical data for various mining regions. My work consistently includes technical development roles across various projects, and more recently, the use of programming to create custom remote sensing tools.

A Geological Service for Europe. Planning and execution of multiple UAV missions (aerophotogrammetry and LiDAR) to gather data on several abandoned mines in southern Portugal. A custom R script was also converted into a user-friendly toolbox for estimating Total Suspended Sediments (TSS).
